I built a small web app to train my ear to recognize musical intervals: it plays two notes on a realistic sampled grand piano — together or one after another — and you name the interval, from unison up through the 13th.

**[Try it here](https://alainfidahoussen.github.io/piano-app-trainer/)**

A few things it supports:

- Harmonic (together), melodic (one after another), or mixed playback, with ascending/descending/random direction
- All 22 interval qualities from unison to major 13th, individually selectable
- A playable on-screen keyboard, for checking your answer by ear
- Score and streak tracking, saved locally in your browser

The [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/AlainFidahoussen/piano-app-trainer) if you're curious how it works.
